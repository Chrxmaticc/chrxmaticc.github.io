faq

questions, answered

everything you'd want to know before hitting invite.

is chromed really free?
yes. every feature on every server. no premium tier, no subscription, no locked commands. free forever.
how do i set it up?
click invite, pick your server, and you're done. it takes about 30 seconds. the setup wizard runs on first join to help you configure everything.
what makes the antinuke special?
most antinukes are just rate limiters. chromed tracks every destructive action with an audit trail, auto-locks during raids, uses honeypot channels to trap attackers, and can restore deleted channels and roles.
how good is the ai?
chromed runs a hybrid model — gemini-tier reasoning with groq speed. it has long-term memory per user, remembers running jokes, and you can customize its personality per server.
can i self-host it?
not right now — chromed runs on our infrastructure so you don't have to maintain anything. self-hosting may come later.
what happens to my data if i remove it?
when you remove chromed, all server config is wiped within 30 days. no backups kept, no shadow profiles.
